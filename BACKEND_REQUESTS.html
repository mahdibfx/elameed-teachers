Done — live on the API and used by the app

Everything in your 9 Oct answer, point by point.

  • Security — every route I flagged now returns 401 without a token: /sessions, /sessions/:id, /groups/:id, /groups/:id/students, /groups/schedules, /students. Attendance writes are limited to the teacher's own groups. Only /rooms is still open, which is just room labels.
  • Cancel with a justification — the teacher can attach a photo or a PDF (camera, gallery or file), sent as multipart.
  • Replacement teacher — typing a name searches /teacher/directory; picking a colleague sends their id, and a free-typed name goes as text. It no longer rides inside the description.
  • 15-minute grace — Cancel stays available until 15 minutes after the start, as the rule now says.
  • Ask to Edit — a held session shows a button that sends the request with an optional reason, then shows "waiting for the office" with a Withdraw button.
  • Problem category — the six problems from the design are sent as category codes instead of the first line of the description.
  • Payments tab — back in the app: total paid, this year, salary terms per group and the payment history.
  • Stopped groups — the Groups screen's Stopped section and Student Info's Completed Groups now fill up, with the real running_since ~ stopped_at period. Their sessions are shown read-only.
  • Push notifications — the app registers the phone on login, removes it on logout, and reloads the sessions and local reminders when the office changes one. It stays switched off until the Firebase file exists (see below).
  • Test account — group 310 has its weekly schedule and session 1406 (Sat 10 Oct, 18:00) is waiting to be tried.

    The app is connected to the Firebase project elameed-system: Android app com.elameed.teachers registered, google-services.json and firebase_options.dart in the repo, Gradle plugin on.

    The phone registers its token fine; it just never receives anything.
